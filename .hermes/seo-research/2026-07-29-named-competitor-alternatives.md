Fair-equivalent rule: match monthly hours, coverage window, assistant location, delivery model, role seniority, management, backup, tools, setup fees, and commitment before comparing price.

Structure: H1, buyer brief, fair-equivalent criteria, alternatives by service model, comparison table, pricing freshness section, decision routine, CTA, five FAQs, TOC, related cards, article and FAQ schema, and deterministic image.

Humanizer rule: direct verbs, concrete buying questions, short and long sentence mix, no invented customer stories, no universal winner, and no unsupported performance claims.

Dash check: no em dash in article prose. Hyphens appear only in compound terms where needed.

Thumbnail rule: zero-based index modulo three in fixed data order: `/va-comparison-team.jpg`, `/thank-you-hero.png`, `/logo.svg`.
