# V3 Design Registry Proposal

## Editorial direction

Reading this as: a practical virtual assistant buyer publication for owner-led teams comparing providers and role models, with a calm evidence-led editorial visual language, leaning toward magazine-led editorial utility.

This is an **evolve** direction rather than a wholesale replacement. The visual system is light, with a restrained rust accent, squared editorial sheets, and rule lines that clarify hierarchy. Newsreader is the display face, paired with a legible system sans serif for navigation, controls, labels, and body copy.

Documentary team photography and workflow imagery should explain who or what is being discussed. They should not function as unsupported proof. Motion remains restrained and functional. Testimonial movement pauses for interaction and becomes a complete static set when reduced motion is requested.

## Registry settings

- `DESIGN_VARIANCE: 8`
- `MOTION_INTENSITY: 2`
- `VISUAL_DENSITY: 7`

## Applied Task 1 decisions

- Sticky, stable shared shell with the subsidiary mark kept distinct from the official Stealth Agents wordmark.
- Three primary destinations: Home, Services, and Contact.
- Visible keyboard focus, skip navigation, mobile disclosure containment, and 44 pixel mobile controls.
- Squared contact form, editorial rules, restrained shadows, and rust action color.
- First party reviews remain verbatim, are labeled as first party, and carry a source link and limitation.
- Official team imagery is identified as official source material rather than independent verification.
