# Best Virtual Assistant Services redesign

Requested full redesign, with the existing article URLs, service depth, lead ingestion, and tracking preserved.

Design references: Instrument (https://www.instrument.com) for confident typography and modular presentation; Ramotion (https://www.ramotion.com) for a restrained centered headline and photographic composition. Original layouts and copy, not copied assets.

Visual system: self-hosted Cabinet Grotesk, warm off-white, forest green and sage. Wide centered hero, photographic service accordion, stacked comparison cards with GSAP ScrollTrigger, existing sourced review marquee, landscape editorial cards, and a separate Parent Company footer block. Reduced-motion users receive static comparison cards and a manually scrollable review list.

Images: 14 distinct locally stored Unsplash photographs. Provenance URLs and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in redesign-image-sources.json. Existing editorial images are retained. No AI-generated imagery was added. This does not claim portfolio-wide photographic uniqueness: the historical article library already reuses source photographs under different title treatments.

Ten service pages retain their responsibilities, boundaries, operating guides, FAQs and related links. Homepage and service-directory heroes use different photographs. Blog cards on the index, pagination pages and homepage use 16:9 frames. Research filters that all linked to the same URL were removed.

Contact retains all fields, submission ownership, backend receipt checking, and ACR-only notification ownership. No production test leads are submitted. The official Bacolod image directly follows the booking section. Header preserves Home, Services and Contact Us, mobile focus management, and sponsorship.

Validation: production Next build, TypeScript, 14 release-contract tests, all 709 raster files decoded, rendered-image path audit, local route HTTP checks, and desktop/mobile browser review. Live release evidence is recorded separately after deployment.
