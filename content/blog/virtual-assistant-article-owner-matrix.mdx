# Create an Owner Matrix for Virtual Assistant Services Articles

Ownership should follow the decision being made, with support roles responsible for preparation and accountable roles responsible for public judgment. This guide keeps daily article creation grounded in a clear reader question, visible evidence, and a practical role boundary.

## Create an Owner Matrix for Virtual Assistant Services Articles: Name the operating decision

Start by writing the decision the record must support. A buyer may be deciding what to delegate, an operator may be preparing a recurring handoff, or a manager may be deciding whether a public article is ready. A virtual assistant services workflow becomes easier to manage when the decision appears before the activity list. Write what evidence would change it and avoid using “in progress” as the only status. This is the practical test for article owner matrix: keep the next handoff visible and prevent activity from being mistaken for approval.

For article owner matrix, record the owner and the evidence that supports the next move. If the context changes, reopen the affected check instead of silently carrying the old assumption into a public article. The record should also preserve the reason for the decision, the date of review, and the exact question that would send the work back to an earlier gate. That detail is valuable when daily article creation is shared across roles or when an article is revisited after a source, audience, or operating assumption changes. It lets a virtual assistant prepare a precise update instead of repeating every prior step.
