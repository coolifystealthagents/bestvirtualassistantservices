# Virtual assistant compliance evidence folders

Link each evidence item to requirement, period, source, scope, verifier, limitation, and retention. The assistant finds and checks records; the owner decides sufficiency and specialist review.

## Applying virtual assistant compliance evidence folders

Evidence is persuasive only when its scope is clear. Link each item to the requirement, period, source, verifier, limitation, and retention rule, and distinguish an absent record from a record that does not prove the claim. A neat folder cannot repair an unsupported conclusion.

## Role boundary and review

The assistant can locate records, normalize names, and flag gaps or scope mismatches. The owner or specialist decides whether the evidence is sufficient and what limitation must be disclosed. Keep the source version and verification date with the item so later reviewers can reproduce the check.

Use the [services library](/services) to separate evidence preparation from assurance decisions, while the [provider vetting guide](/provider-vetting) frames review ownership. [NIST guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber) is a useful access-control reference.

### Evidence index

Keep requirement, period, source, scope, verifier, limitation, retention rule, and last verification date.
