# Virtual assistant compliance evidence folders

Link each evidence item to requirement, period, source, scope, verifier, limitation, and retention. The assistant finds and checks records; the owner decides sufficiency and specialist review.

## Applying virtual assistant compliance evidence folders

For virtual assistant compliance evidence folders, begin with the authoritative source and write down the known facts, missing inputs, and due point. Test one ordinary case and one exception before calling the process routine. Keep an unresolved item visible instead of turning an assumption into completion.

## Role boundary and review

In virtual assistant compliance evidence folders, the virtual assistant may gather context, normalize records, identify gaps, and prepare a review queue. A usable handoff names the evidence, open question, next owner, and review date.
