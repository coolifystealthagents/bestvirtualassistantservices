# Writing a Project Status Summary People Can Act On

A useful status summary tells readers what changed, what is at risk, what needs a decision, and what happens next.

## Writing a Project Status Summary People Can Act On begins with the right facts

Use evidence from the agreed project record. Separate completed work, current blockers, upcoming commitments, and decisions needed from the owner. Use the [work intake guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-work-intake-triage) to state the requested outcome, relevant deadline, and decision owner before details are gathered. A focused brief keeps support work useful without asking for information that does not affect the next step.

## Check context before changing a record

Compare the current record with the approved source and note the date checked. Keep original wording when it carries meaning, and distinguish a confirmed fact from a suggestion or interpretation. If two sources disagree, preserve both references and make the conflict visible.

## Keep authority and uncertainty visible

Do not hide a missed commitment, change a status to make it look better, or assign blame without evidence. Escalate a material risk as soon as it is clear. A virtual assistant can prepare information, organize a review list, and point out missing evidence; the owner or designated specialist should make decisions that change commitments, public statements, access, or sensitive records.

## Make the handoff easy to review

Keep the summary short enough to read quickly, with links to supporting records. Give every open decision an owner and a date for review. A [document management guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-document-management-system) can keep the approved source separate from working notes. For general security considerations when handling business records, consult the [NIST small business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber).

## Questions worth answering before the work is closed

### What is the source of truth?

Name the record or approved document that controls the decision, and record when it was last checked.

### What belongs in an exception note?

State the missing or conflicting fact, the evidence reviewed, the risk of guessing, and who should decide.

### When is the handoff ready?

It is ready when completed items, unresolved questions, source links, and the next decision owner are all clear.
