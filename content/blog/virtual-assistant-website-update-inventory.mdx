# An Inventory for Website Updates That Prevents Missed Pages

A website update inventory shows which pages, links, images, and supporting records are affected by a requested change.

## An Inventory for Website Updates That Prevents Missed Pages begins with the right facts

Define the requested outcome, list the affected URLs, note the source of each replacement detail, and identify pages that may reference the same fact. Record checks separately from approval. Use the [work intake guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-project-status-reporting) to state the requested outcome, relevant deadline, and decision owner before details are gathered. A focused brief keeps support work useful without asking for information that does not affect the next step.

## Check context before changing a record

Compare the current record with the approved source and note the date checked. Keep original wording when it carries meaning, and distinguish a confirmed fact from a suggestion or interpretation. If two sources disagree, preserve both references and make the conflict visible.

## Keep authority and uncertainty visible

Do not change public copy, remove a page, or claim that a link is fixed without the authorized reviewer’s decision and evidence. Flag redirects and accessibility concerns for the right specialist. A virtual assistant can prepare information, organize a review list, and point out missing evidence; the owner or designated specialist should make decisions that change commitments, public statements, access, or sensitive records.

## Make the handoff easy to review

Return a page-by-page summary with proposed change, source, reviewer, and unresolved risk. This helps an owner approve a bounded set of edits without losing the wider context. A [document management guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-work-intake-triage) can keep the approved source separate from working notes. For general security considerations when handling business records, consult the [NIST small business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber).

## Questions worth answering before the work is closed

### What is the source of truth?

Name the record or approved document that controls the decision, and record when it was last checked.

### What belongs in an exception note?

State the missing or conflicting fact, the evidence reviewed, the risk of guessing, and who should decide.

### When is the handoff ready?

It is ready when completed items, unresolved questions, source links, and the next decision owner are all clear.
