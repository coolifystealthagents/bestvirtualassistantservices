# Field Guide: Knowledge-Base Article Reviews

Knowledge-Base Article Reviews work best when an owner can see current status, supporting evidence, and the next decision. This guide focuses on a practical record and a clean handoff.

## Field Guide: Knowledge-Base Article Reviews starts with a reliable source of truth

Check title, audience, answer, steps, links, owner, review date, and escalation route. Name the authoritative record before collecting secondary details and note when it was checked. Keep incomplete information pending rather than turning assumptions into completed items.

## Keep preparation separate from decisions

Do not invent behavior or silently rewrite policy; send conflicts to the subject owner. Administrative support can gather context, normalize labels, identify missing evidence, and prepare a review queue. The accountable owner retains decisions affecting commitments, sensitive information, public statements, or irreversible changes.

## Make the handoff reproducible

At handoff, include completed items, unresolved questions, source links, next owner, and review date. An exception note should say what is unusual, what was verified, and what remains. ## Frequently asked questions

### What should be recorded first?

Record the intended outcome, source of truth, owner, and approval boundary.

### How should uncertainty be handled?

Label it plainly, preserve evidence, and route the decision to the person with authority.

### When is the record ready to share?

When status, open questions, sources, and the next action are clear without reconstructing the conversation.
