# Virtual Assistant Employee Records Administration

For employee records administration, begin with the record that makes the next decision possible, then keep the owner and stopping point visible.

## Virtual Assistant Employee Records Administration: Classify the record before handling it

Employee records can include routine administration, payroll information, performance material, health information, or legal correspondence. Identify the category before sharing or storing anything. The classification determines who needs access and which retention rule may apply.

## Use a source-of-truth checklist

For each record, capture the employee identifier, document type, effective date, owner, and location of the authoritative copy. Do not use an email attachment as the official record merely because it arrived first. A checklist exposes missing context before a file is filed or forwarded.

## Keep access role-based

People should see the records required for their responsibilities, not every record in the folder. Separate administrative updates from sensitive material and review shared links periodically. If access is uncertain, pause and ask the designated owner rather than guessing from job title.

## Handle changes with an audit note

When a record is corrected, retain the original where policy requires it and state what changed, why, and who approved the change. Do not silently overwrite a historical employment fact. A concise audit note protects both the employee and the organization’s ability to explain its records.

## Respect retention and requests

Keep records according to the organization’s documented retention schedule and applicable obligations. Route legal holds, employee requests, and uncertain deletion questions to the responsible specialist. The [Department of Labor records page](https://www.dol.gov/general/aboutdol/foia/records) provides useful context for why records governance matters.


## Keep the decision visible

For this topic, preserve the source and route uncertainty to the named owner. Reference: [U.S. Department of Labor](https://www.dol.gov/general/aboutdol/foia/records).
