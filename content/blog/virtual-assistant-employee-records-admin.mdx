# Virtual Assistant Employee Records Administration

A boundary-conscious guide to organizing employee records, reminders, and missing information without making employment decisions.

## Virtual Assistant Employee Records Administration: Define the record and the authority

List the approved record system, required fields, retention rule, and responsible owner. Separate routine filing from decisions about employment, leave, compensation, discipline, or accommodations. The assistant needs a clear escalation route for anything sensitive or disputed.

## Use a missing-document queue

Record which item is missing, who owns the next action, and the due date. A virtual assistant can send an approved reminder and keep a status log, but should not interpret policy or make a personnel decision. The document review guide helps keep completeness checks distinct from judgment.

## Limit access and review changes

Use least access, named accounts, and a record of approvals. Review temporary folders and remove duplicates after the approved filing step. NIST small-business guidance is a useful starting point for account and information protection: [NIST small-business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber)

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the owner provide first?

Provide the source of truth, approved examples, the expected output, the reviewer, and a clear rule for exceptions.

### What should remain with the owner?

Keep approvals, policy interpretation, sensitive decisions, and irreversible changes with the responsible owner.
