# Virtual Assistant Inventory Reorder Tracker

Published August 31, 2026.

An inventory assistant can keep evidence current while purchasing and forecasting decisions remain with the business owner.

## Virtual Assistant Inventory Reorder Tracker: define reorder inputs

Record item identifiers, approved vendors, count source, lead time, threshold, and decision owner. Compare operational roles in the [service role library](/services).

## Reconcile counts before alerting

Check timestamps, returns, damaged stock, open orders, and duplicate entries. Label estimates clearly and do not silently replace source-system values.

## Route purchase decisions

Prepare a reorder packet with quantity on hand, threshold, open orders, and exception notes. Protect vendor and account information using the [NIST small business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber).

## Close the inventory loop

After approval, record the authorized action and expected arrival without modifying price or quantity independently. The [provider vetting checklist](/provider-vetting) helps assess operational controls.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the assistant document?

Document the source, action, status, owner, due date, and any exception that needs review.

### When should work stop?

Stop when evidence is missing, instructions conflict, access is inappropriate, or an owner-only decision is required.
