# Virtual assistant recruiting schedule coordination



## How to manage Virtual assistant recruiting schedule coordination

Capture interviewer availability, participants, time zones, preferences, links, and changes. Keep evaluation and selection with the hiring owner.

## Role boundary for Virtual assistant recruiting schedule coordination

Maintain one current invitation and route accommodation or employment concerns to the designated recruiting owner.

## Evidence and handoff

Use the [services library](/services) and [provider vetting](/provider-vetting) to define ownership. For a named reference, consult [EEOC employer guidance](https://www.eeoc.gov/employers).

## FAQ

### What should the owner record?
The source, reviewer, next action, and the point at which the virtual assistant must stop and escalate.
