# Virtual assistant recruiting schedule coordination

Represent candidate, interviewer, time zone, preparation, confirmation, and decision owner separately. The assistant coordinates logistics; the hiring owner retains evaluation and accommodation decisions.

## Applying virtual assistant recruiting schedule coordination

Scheduling interviews is a coordination task with a human decision at the end. Capture candidate and interviewer time zones, preparation status, accessibility needs, confirmation, and the decision owner. A filled calendar slot is not the same as a confirmed, usable interview.

## Role boundary and review

The assistant can offer windows, confirm logistics, and flag a missing response. The hiring owner retains candidate evaluation, accommodation decisions, and any change to the process. Keep declined, expired, and rescheduled slots visible so the schedule does not overstate recruiting progress.
