# Virtual Assistant Source Capture Routine for Daily Articles

Published September 1, 2026.

Source capture turns browsing into an auditable editorial input instead of a collection of disconnected tabs.
Use the [content calendar workflow](/blog/virtual-assistant-content-calendar-workflow) to place this control in the daily sequence, the [editorial quality checklist](/blog/virtual-assistant-editorial-quality-checklist) for adjacent review steps, and [NIST small business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber) when the routine handles business information.

## Virtual Assistant Source Capture Routine for Daily Articles: operating steps

List the factual questions the article must answer and the claims that need evidence. Search against those questions, recording unresolved gaps rather than filling them with assumptions.

## Save context with every source

For each source, record its title, publisher, URL, publication or update date, access date, relevant section, and the claim it supports. A bare link is difficult for an editor to evaluate later.

## Prefer original authority

Use official documentation, public agencies, standards bodies, and original research where they fit the question. Secondary explanations can help orientation, but they should not silently replace the underlying evidence.

## Hand off limits as clearly as findings

Note disagreements, stale figures, unavailable documents, geographic limits, and uncertain definitions. The writer needs to know what the research cannot establish as much as what it can.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many sources are enough?

There is no universal count. Coverage is sufficient when each material claim has appropriate support and important limitations remain visible.

### May a virtual assistant decide which disputed source is correct?

The assistant can compare provenance and dates, but disputed editorial judgments should go to the named reviewer.
