# An Evidence Index for Customer Returns

A returns evidence index keeps each case understandable without making a support coordinator decide whether a refund or exception is warranted.

## An Evidence Index for Customer Returns starts with a reliable record

Link the case identifier to the order record, customer message, item details, dates, and current status. Preserve the original evidence and label staff summaries as summaries. Name the source of truth before work begins, record when it was checked, and make the intended outcome explicit. This keeps a small administrative task from becoming a chain of assumptions.

## Separate preparation from approval

Escalate safety claims, disputed receipt information, and policy exceptions. The index should help the authorized reviewer decide, not make that decision by implication. A virtual assistant can gather details, normalize labels, identify missing fields, and prepare a review queue. The owner or designated specialist should retain decisions that affect commitments, sensitive information, public statements, or irreversible changes.

## Make the handoff easy to review

At handoff, include the completed range, unresolved items, source links, and a short explanation of unusual cases. ## Frequently asked questions

### What should be checked first?

Confirm the source of truth, the intended audience, the required output, and the approval boundary.

### What belongs in an exception note?

State what is unusual, what evidence was checked, what remains uncertain, and who must decide the next step.

### When is the task ready to expand?

Expand only after review shows that the instructions, records, and escalation path work together consistently.
