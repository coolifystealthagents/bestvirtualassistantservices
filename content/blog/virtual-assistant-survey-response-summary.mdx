# Virtual Assistant Survey Response Summaries

For survey response summaries, begin with the record that makes the next decision possible, then keep the owner and stopping point visible.

## Virtual Assistant Survey Response Summaries: State what the survey can answer

Open with the audience, field period, number of responses, and questions the survey was designed to inform. A summary becomes misleading when readers assume it represents a broader group than the method supports. Scope is part of the finding, not a footnote.

## Separate counts from themes

Report the response count and notable patterns before interpreting them. For open text, describe the coding approach and include representative paraphrases rather than presenting one vivid comment as typical. Keep rare but important concerns visible without inflating their frequency.

## Show uncertainty honestly

Call out missing responses, skipped questions, small subgroups, and wording that may have influenced answers. Avoid ranking groups when the difference is too small or the sample is not comparable. A careful summary earns more trust than a confident headline.

## Connect findings to decisions

For each meaningful pattern, explain what it suggests the organization should examine next. Do not turn preference into proof that a change will work. Recommend a follow-up question, test, or conversation where the evidence stops.

## Preserve the source

Keep the questionnaire, field dates, response data, coding notes, and version of the summary linked together. The [Pew Research Center survey methods resources](https://www.pewresearch.org/methods/u-s-survey-research/) provide useful background for communicating survey limits.


## Keep the decision visible

For this topic, preserve the source and route uncertainty to the named owner. Reference: [Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/methods/u-s-survey-research/).
