## A closing checklist for survey response summaries

Ask what would make this case unsafe to close. It may be a missing question wording, conflicting records, or a request beyond the agreed role. Write that condition into the handoff. Clear boundaries let a virtual assistant move quickly on routine work and stop early when judgment is required.
One final review of survey response summaries should ask whether the record tells the truth about its state. It should distinguish a verified fact from a proposed correction, a completed action from a waiting action, and a normal case from an exception. Those distinctions are especially important when a serious rare complaint disappears beneath a common answer.
If the work is handed between people, preserve the source and the question together. A later reviewer should not have to reconstruct why a field was accepted, why a correction was declined, or why an item remained open. This simple record of reasoning protects continuity and makes the service easier to improve.
