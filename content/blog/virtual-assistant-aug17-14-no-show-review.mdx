# Virtual assistant appointment no-show reviews



## How to manage Virtual assistant appointment no-show reviews

Reconcile scheduled time, time zone, confirmations, cancellations, and stated reason before suggesting a cause. An absence does not prove intent.

## Role boundary for Virtual assistant appointment no-show reviews

Choose a defined period and one bounded reminder change; record the result and its limits, especially where accessibility may matter.

## Evidence and handoff

Use the [services library](/services) and [provider vetting](/provider-vetting) to define ownership. For a named reference, consult [DOJ accessibility resources](https://www.ada.gov/resources/).

## FAQ

### What should the owner record?
The source, reviewer, next action, and the point at which the virtual assistant must stop and escalate.
