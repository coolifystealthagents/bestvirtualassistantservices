# Virtual assistant appointment no-show reviews

Distinguish no-show, cancellation, reschedule, wrong contact, and record error. Check calendar, reminders, time zone, and policy before inferring a reason or trend.

## Applying virtual assistant appointment no-show reviews

Review the event trail before naming a no-show: invitation, reminder, time zone, cancellation, reschedule, contact detail, and attendance record can disagree. Use a neutral reason code for “not enough evidence” so a single scheduling error is not converted into a customer or staff performance judgment.

## Role boundary and review

The assistant can reconcile timestamps and prepare a factual review. The owner decides whether a policy action, follow-up, or correction is appropriate. Trend reports should distinguish confirmed no-shows from cancellations and record errors before changing reminder or scheduling practice.

Use the [services library](/services) to separate calendar administration from customer-policy decisions, while the [provider vetting guide](/provider-vetting) frames review ownership. [NIST guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber) is a useful access baseline.

### Review record

Keep invitation, reminder, time zone, attendance evidence, neutral reason code, owner decision, and trend classification.
