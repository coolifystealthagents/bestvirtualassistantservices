# Virtual Assistant Travel Expense Reconciliation

Published August 31, 2026.

Travel reconciliation support can organize evidence and expose exceptions while financial decisions remain owner-controlled.

## Virtual Assistant Travel Expense Reconciliation: assemble the trip packet

Collect the approved itinerary, booking confirmations, receipts, credits, policy, and approver details. The [service role library](/services) shows related administrative lanes.

## Match records line by line

Connect each charge to a traveler, date, purpose, and receipt. Flag currency conversions, missing evidence, cancellations, and duplicate amounts.

## Protect financial information

Use approved storage, named accounts, and least-necessary access. The [NIST small business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber) provides a useful security baseline.

## Route exceptions and close

Send unclear items to the authorized reviewer and preserve the approval trail. The [provider vetting checklist](/provider-vetting) can guide questions about finance-adjacent support controls.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the assistant document?

Document the source, action, status, owner, due date, and any exception that needs review.

### When should work stop?

Stop when evidence is missing, instructions conflict, access is inappropriate, or an owner-only decision is required.
