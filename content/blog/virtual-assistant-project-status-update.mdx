# Virtual Assistant Project Status Updates

For project status updates, begin with the record that makes the next decision possible, then keep the owner and stopping point visible.

## Virtual Assistant Project Status Updates: Report the project’s position

A status update should let a reader answer four questions quickly: what moved, what is next, what is at risk, and what decision is needed. Put the reporting period and overall status at the top. A clear position is more valuable than a long list of activity.

## Describe progress through outcomes

Name the deliverable or decision completed, not only the meeting held or file touched. Include the evidence a reader can inspect. If work is underway but not usable yet, say so. This keeps effort from being mistaken for progress.

## Surface risk early

For each risk, state the trigger, likely effect, owner, and response under consideration. Separate an active issue from a possibility. Do not lower a risk rating merely because no one has complained. Early visibility gives the owner more choices.

## Ask for decisions precisely

A decision request should include the options, recommendation if one exists, deadline, and consequence of waiting. Avoid asking a broad question such as what do you think. Specific decisions produce faster and more accountable replies.

## Keep the history useful

Use a consistent format, preserve prior updates, and explain changes in forecast or scope. The [Project Management Institute communication research](https://www.pmi.org/learning/library/project-communications-management-communication-9097) underscores why communication needs a deliberate audience and purpose.


## Keep the decision visible

For this topic, preserve the source and route uncertainty to the named owner.
