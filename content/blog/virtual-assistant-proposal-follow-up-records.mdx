# How to Keep Proposal Follow-Up Records Useful

A follow-up record should show what was sent, what the prospective client asked, what remains unanswered, and when the next contact is appropriate.

## How to Keep Proposal Follow-Up Records Useful begins with the right facts

Record the proposal version, delivery date, recipient, requested response, and any stated concern. Link to the approved proposal rather than copying sensitive details into a second system. Use the [work intake guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-client-onboarding-controls) to state the requested outcome, relevant deadline, and decision owner before details are gathered. A focused brief keeps support work useful without asking for information that does not affect the next step.

## Check context before changing a record

Compare the current record with the approved source and note the date checked. Keep original wording when it carries meaning, and distinguish a confirmed fact from a suggestion or interpretation. If two sources disagree, preserve both references and make the conflict visible.

## Keep authority and uncertainty visible

Do not pressure a recipient, invent urgency, or mark a proposal as accepted without clear evidence. Respect an explicit request not to be contacted. A virtual assistant can prepare information, organize a review list, and point out missing evidence; the owner or designated specialist should make decisions that change commitments, public statements, access, or sensitive records.

## Make the handoff easy to review

Summarize the current state in plain language and make the next action conditional on the owner’s direction. This keeps follow-up timely without making it mechanical or intrusive. A [document management guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-project-status-reporting) can keep the approved source separate from working notes. For general security considerations when handling business records, consult the [NIST small business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber).

## Questions worth answering before the work is closed

### What is the source of truth?

Name the record or approved document that controls the decision, and record when it was last checked.

### What belongs in an exception note?

State the missing or conflicting fact, the evidence reviewed, the risk of guessing, and who should decide.

### When is the handoff ready?

It is ready when completed items, unresolved questions, source links, and the next decision owner are all clear.
