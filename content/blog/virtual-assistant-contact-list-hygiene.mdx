# Virtual Assistant Contact List Hygiene

For contact list hygiene, begin with the record that makes the next decision possible, then keep the owner and stopping point visible.

## Virtual Assistant Contact List Hygiene: Define a trustworthy contact

A contact entry should tell a future reader who the person is, why the business keeps the record, and which source supplied the details. Use a stable identifier such as the organization plus email address when names repeat. Avoid collecting every possible field simply because a system offers one.

## Normalize the details people actually use

Choose one format for names, phone numbers, organization names, and roles. Keep the original value when a distinction matters, such as a preferred name or a regional phone extension. Consistency makes search and matching easier, but normalization should never erase useful context.

## Find duplicates with evidence

Compare likely matches using more than a display name. Check the email domain, organization, phone suffix, and last-confirmed date. If the match is uncertain, place the entries in a review list rather than merging them. A false merge can redirect a private message to the wrong person.

## Record consent and communication preference

A clean list is not automatically a permission list. Keep the source of an opt-in, opt-out, or channel preference where the business relies on it. Honor suppression requests promptly, and avoid treating an old relationship as unlimited permission. The FTC’s [CAN-SPAM compliance guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business) is a useful starting point for commercial email practices.

## Set a maintenance rhythm

Review bounced addresses, role changes, duplicate candidates, and stale records on a defined cadence. Let the review produce a short exception queue with a reason for each item. That queue is easier to work than a broad instruction to clean the database, and it makes unresolved uncertainty visible.


## Keep the decision visible

For this topic, preserve the source and route uncertainty to the named owner.
