# Virtual Assistant Client Onboarding Checklist

Published August 31, 2026.

A client onboarding assistant keeps intake moving without making promises or changing scope.

## Virtual Assistant Client Onboarding Checklist: define the onboarding record

Create one approved record for contact details, purchased service, target dates, dependencies, and the accountable owner. Use the [service role library](/services) to keep the assigned work within a clear role lane.

## Control requests and approvals

Separate information collection from decisions. The assistant may chase missing fields, schedule approved meetings, and log status, while pricing, scope, legal terms, and exceptions remain with the owner.

## Protect access during setup

Grant only the systems needed for the current stage. The [NIST small business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber) offers a practical baseline for accounts, access, and business information.

## Close with a verified handoff

Confirm that required fields are complete, the owner has accepted the record, and the client knows the next approved step. The [provider vetting checklist](/provider-vetting) helps teams evaluate how a service handles onboarding and oversight.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the assistant document?

Document the source, action, status, owner, due date, and any exception that needs review.

### When should work stop?

Stop when evidence is missing, instructions conflict, access is inappropriate, or an owner-only decision is required.
