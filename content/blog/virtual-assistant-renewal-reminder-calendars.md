# Virtual Assistant Renewal Reminder Calendars

A renewal calendar should create decision time, not just announce a deadline. It needs enough context for an owner to review the relationship before an automatic date becomes an urgent problem.

## Virtual Assistant Renewal Reminder Calendars: map the lead time

Record the renewal date, notice period, review date, and the date by which a decision must be communicated. Label the source for each date because different agreements may use different terms.

## Add a preparation checklist

List the records needed for review, the person who owns the decision, and questions that may affect continuation. Keep the checklist separate from the calendar title so it remains searchable.

## Use meaningful reminder states

Choose states such as information gathering, owner review, decision pending, and completed. A reminder should not be marked done merely because it was read.

## Escalate ambiguity

If the date or authority is unclear, surface the question with its source. The [renewal calendar guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-service-renewal-calendar) supports cadence, while the [vendor renewal guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-vendor-renewal-tracking) adds relationship context.

## Secure the record

Avoid putting confidential contract terms in a shared title. Review [NIST small business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber) when setting access to the underlying documents.
