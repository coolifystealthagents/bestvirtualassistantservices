# Virtual assistant meeting cadence

Define purpose, participants, input, decision owner, frequency, and cancellation rule. Review decision latency, repeated agenda items, and stalled actions before keeping a recurring meeting.

## Applying virtual assistant meeting cadence

For virtual assistant meeting cadence, begin with the authoritative source and write down the known facts, missing inputs, and due point. Test one ordinary case and one exception before calling the process routine. Keep an unresolved item visible instead of turning an assumption into completion.

## Role boundary and review

In virtual assistant meeting cadence, the virtual assistant may gather context, normalize records, identify gaps, and prepare a review queue. The owner retains decisions involving commitments, sensitive information, public statements, legal interpretation, or irreversible changes. A usable handoff names the evidence, open question, next owner, and review date.
