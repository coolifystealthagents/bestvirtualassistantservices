# Virtual assistant meeting cadence



## How to manage Virtual assistant meeting cadence

A cadence should explain what changes between meetings: queue health, deadlines, blocked inputs, quality exceptions, or decisions. If nothing changes, reduce the meeting.

## Role boundary for Virtual assistant meeting cadence

Use written status for completed work and open questions; reserve live time for ambiguity and trade-offs.

### What should the owner record?
The source, reviewer, next action, and the point at which the virtual assistant must stop and escalate.
