# Virtual Assistant Community Moderation Playbook

Published August 31, 2026.

A moderation assistant needs clear rules, evidence standards, and an immediate path for high-risk situations.

## Virtual Assistant Community Moderation Playbook: define the moderation matrix

Map common violations to approved actions, evidence requirements, response templates, and escalation owners. Review role scope in the [service role library](/services).

## Process the queue consistently

Read the full context, apply the written rule, preserve required evidence, and log the action. Avoid improvising policy or engaging in arguments.

## Escalate urgent risks

Route threats, self-harm indicators, illegal content, privacy incidents, and unclear edge cases immediately. The [NIST small business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber) supports responsible account and information controls.

## Review patterns without overreach

Summarize volumes, response times, appeals, and recurring ambiguity for the policy owner. The [provider vetting checklist](/provider-vetting) helps assess training and supervision.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the assistant document?

Document the source, action, status, owner, due date, and any exception that needs review.

### When should work stop?

Stop when evidence is missing, instructions conflict, access is inappropriate, or an owner-only decision is required.
