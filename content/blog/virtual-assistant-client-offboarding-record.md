# Keeping a Client Offboarding Record Complete

A client offboarding record documents what was returned, closed, transferred, or left for a named owner.

## Keeping a Client Offboarding Record Complete starts with a reliable record

List active access, open commitments, final deliverables, data-return instructions, contact preferences, and the approval for each closure step. Keep dates and evidence together. Name the source of truth before work begins, record when it was checked, and make the intended outcome explicit. This keeps a small administrative task from becoming a chain of assumptions.

## Separate preparation from approval

Do not delete records, revoke access, or promise retention terms without authorization. Sensitive account and legal questions belong with the designated owner. A virtual assistant can gather details, normalize labels, identify missing fields, and prepare a review queue. The owner or designated specialist should retain decisions that affect commitments, sensitive information, public statements, or irreversible changes.

## Make the handoff easy to review

At handoff, include the completed range, unresolved items, source links, and a short explanation of unusual cases. ## Frequently asked questions

### What should be checked first?

Confirm the source of truth, the intended audience, the required output, and the approval boundary.

### What belongs in an exception note?

State what is unusual, what evidence was checked, what remains uncertain, and who must decide the next step.

### When is the task ready to expand?

Expand only after review shows that the instructions, records, and escalation path work together consistently.
