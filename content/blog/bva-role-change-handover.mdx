## Where the process usually breaks

For the operations manager, the handover lists tasks but not the promises attached to them. Access is transferred without removing the old holder, creating two active owners. The incoming owner changes a decision rule without recording the change. These are ordinary failure modes, not evidence of bad intent.

For Best Virtual Assistant Services's virtual assistant placement and managed services team, They appear when the queue is measured by activity and the evidence standard is assumed. The fix is usually a smaller checklist and a clearer stop rule, not more reminders.
