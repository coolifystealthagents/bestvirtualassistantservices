# Virtual Assistant Vendor Renewal Calendar

Published August 31, 2026.

A renewal calendar prevents missed notice windows while keeping contract interpretation and commercial decisions with authorized owners.

## Virtual Assistant Vendor Renewal Calendar: build the renewal register

Record vendor, service, term dates, notice window, owner, source document, and status. Use the [service role library](/services) to keep administration distinct from negotiation.

## Verify dates against source documents

Double-check every date and record where it came from. Flag conflicting terms rather than choosing an interpretation.

## Send staged reminders

Schedule reminders far enough ahead for review and route commercial or legal questions to the owner. Protect contracts and account data using the [NIST small business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber).

## Record the owner decision

Log renew, cancel, renegotiate, or pending only after authorized direction. The [provider vetting checklist](/provider-vetting) helps assess document and escalation controls.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the assistant document?

Document the source, action, status, owner, due date, and any exception that needs review.

### When should work stop?

Stop when evidence is missing, instructions conflict, access is inappropriate, or an owner-only decision is required.
