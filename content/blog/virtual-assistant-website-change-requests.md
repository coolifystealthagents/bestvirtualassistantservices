# Virtual Assistant Website Change Requests

Website requests are easier to execute when the requested outcome, affected location, supplied material, and approval owner are visible before work begins. A short intake prevents a small edit from becoming an unexamined redesign.

## Virtual Assistant Website Change Requests: describe the outcome

Ask what a visitor should see or be able to do after the change. Capture the current URL, requested copy or asset, and any deadline with its source.

## Separate content from behavior

Label whether the request changes wording, layout, links, forms, or access. Different change types need different reviewers and different checks.

## Define acceptance evidence

State what the owner will inspect, such as a heading, link destination, or responsive view. The [website change review guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-website-change-review) supports review, while the [content update guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-website-content-update) covers routine edits.

## Pause for unclear authority

Do not publish a request that changes a legal statement, customer promise, or access control without the named owner’s approval.

## Protect credentials and customer data

Keep access details out of the request body. Review [NIST small business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber) when deciding who needs access to the site or its records.
