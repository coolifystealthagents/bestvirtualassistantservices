# Virtual Assistant Secure Account Offboarding

For secure account offboarding, begin with the record that makes the next decision possible, then keep the owner and stopping point visible.

## Virtual Assistant Secure Account Offboarding: List accounts before closing access

Offboarding starts with an inventory of email, shared storage, calendars, customer systems, devices, password managers, integrations, and recovery methods. Use the person’s responsibilities to find accounts, but confirm against an authoritative access list. Unknown accounts are the main reason an apparently complete departure leaves exposure behind.

## Preserve business records first

Before disabling access, identify records the organization must retain and transfer ownership using approved controls. Do not copy private material indiscriminately. Keep a note of what moved, where it lives, and who accepted responsibility.

## Revoke sessions and pathways

Disable the identity, expire active sessions, remove group membership, rotate shared secrets, and review forwarding or delegated access. Sequence the steps so a person cannot regain entry through an overlooked recovery route. Each system should have a named confirmer.

## Separate departure from deletion

Closing access does not automatically mean deleting records, devices, or mail. Apply the organization’s retention and legal-hold rules, and route uncertainty to the appropriate owner. A rushed deletion can destroy evidence or business history.

## Confirm the result

Record the time, systems checked, exceptions, and unresolved follow-up. Review high-risk systems again after the first business day for unexpected activity. CISA’s [ransomware guide](https://www.cisa.gov/stopransomware/ransomware-guide) reinforces the importance of access control, backups, and recovery planning.


## Keep the decision visible

For this topic, preserve the source and route uncertainty to the named owner. Reference: [Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency](https://www.cisa.gov/stopransomware/ransomware-guide).
