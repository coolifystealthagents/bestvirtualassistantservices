# Virtual assistant role scorecards



## How to manage Virtual assistant role scorecards

A scorecard should name recurring outcomes, inputs, quality checks, escalation triggers, and reserved decisions. “Be proactive” needs concrete examples to guide work.

## Role boundary for Virtual assistant role scorecards

Review the written role against the real queue and add responsibility only with an owner, source, reviewer, and acceptance test.

## Evidence and handoff

Use the [services library](/services) and [provider vetting](/provider-vetting) to define ownership. For a named reference, consult [CIPD performance management factsheet](https://www.cipd.org/en/knowledge/factsheets/performance-factsheet/).

## FAQ

### What should the owner record?
The source, reviewer, next action, and the point at which the virtual assistant must stop and escalate.
