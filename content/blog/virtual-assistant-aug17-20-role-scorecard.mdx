# Virtual assistant role scorecards

Connect responsibilities to accepted outputs, evidence, cadence, escalation triggers, and owner decisions. Use reproducible measures and record context instead of scoring activity alone.

## Applying virtual assistant role scorecards

For virtual assistant role scorecards, begin with the authoritative source and write down the known facts, missing inputs, and due point. Test one ordinary case and one exception before calling the process routine. Keep an unresolved item visible instead of turning an assumption into completion.

## Role boundary and review

In virtual assistant role scorecards, the virtual assistant may gather context, normalize records, identify gaps, and prepare a review queue. The owner retains decisions involving commitments, sensitive information, public statements, legal interpretation, or irreversible changes. A usable handoff names the evidence, open question, next owner, and review date.

### What should be retained?

Keep the source request, reviewer, status or decision, evidence, next action, and escalation point.
