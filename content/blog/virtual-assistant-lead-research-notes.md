# Virtual Assistant Lead Research Notes

Research notes are useful when they show where an observation came from and what remains unknown. They should help an owner decide whether to investigate further, not quietly turn public information into a sales conclusion.

## Virtual Assistant Lead Research Notes: record the source

Capture the page, date checked, organization name as shown, and the specific fact observed. Quote only what is necessary and keep interpretation in a separate field.

## Use cautious labels

Mark information as confirmed, reported, or needing verification. Do not infer budget, intent, or authority from a job title, website design, or social activity.

## Keep records deduplicated

Use a stable organization identifier and compare the source before creating a second record. The [research source log](/blog/virtual-assistant-research-source-log) supports traceability, while the [CRM data entry guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-crm-data-entry-checklist) supports clean capture.

## Define the handoff

State the open question, suggested next research step, and person who decides whether outreach is appropriate. A prepared note is not an instruction to contact anyone.

## Respect public information boundaries

Collect only what is relevant and permitted. [NIST small business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber) is useful when deciding where research notes should be stored.
