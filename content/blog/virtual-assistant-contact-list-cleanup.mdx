# A Safer Approach to Contact List Cleanup

Contact cleanup should improve findability while protecting the history and distinctions that make a record trustworthy.

## A Safer Approach to Contact List Cleanup begins with the right facts

Begin with duplicate candidates based on more than a matching name. Compare organization, role, contact route, last confirmed date, and notes before proposing a merge or archive. Use the [work intake guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-project-status-reporting) to state the requested outcome, relevant deadline, and decision owner before details are gathered. A focused brief keeps support work useful without asking for information that does not affect the next step.

## Check context before changing a record

Compare the current record with the approved source and note the date checked. Keep original wording when it carries meaning, and distinguish a confirmed fact from a suggestion or interpretation. If two sources disagree, preserve both references and make the conflict visible.

## Keep authority and uncertainty visible

Never delete a record because it looks old or incomplete. Keep uncertain entries in a review group and preserve the source record until the owner decides. A virtual assistant can prepare information, organize a review list, and point out missing evidence; the owner or designated specialist should make decisions that change commitments, public statements, access, or sensitive records.

## Make the handoff easy to review

Report the number of clear corrections, possible duplicates, and records needing an owner decision. Include examples without exposing private contact details. A [document management guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-work-intake-triage) can keep the approved source separate from working notes. For general security considerations when handling business records, consult the [NIST small business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber).

## Questions worth answering before the work is closed

### What is the source of truth?

Name the record or approved document that controls the decision, and record when it was last checked.

### What belongs in an exception note?

State the missing or conflicting fact, the evidence reviewed, the risk of guessing, and who should decide.

### When is the handoff ready?

It is ready when completed items, unresolved questions, source links, and the next decision owner are all clear.
