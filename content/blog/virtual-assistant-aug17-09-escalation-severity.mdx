# Virtual assistant escalation severity

Define severity using consequence, urgency, safety, access, customer impact, deadline, and reversibility. The assistant gathers facts; the accountable owner chooses response and records the change.

## Applying virtual assistant escalation severity

For virtual assistant escalation severity, begin with the authoritative source and write down the known facts, missing inputs, and due point. Test one ordinary case and one exception before calling the process routine. Keep an unresolved item visible instead of turning an assumption into completion.

## Role boundary and review

In virtual assistant escalation severity, the virtual assistant may gather context, normalize records, identify gaps, and prepare a review queue. The owner retains decisions involving commitments, sensitive information, public statements, legal interpretation, or irreversible changes. A usable handoff names the evidence, open question, next owner, and review date.
