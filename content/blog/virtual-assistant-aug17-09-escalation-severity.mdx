# Virtual assistant escalation severity

Define severity using consequence, urgency, safety, access, customer impact, deadline, and reversibility. The assistant gathers facts; the accountable owner chooses response and records the change.

## Applying virtual assistant escalation severity

Severity should reflect consequence and reversibility, not the loudness of the message. Ask what could be harmed, how soon action is needed, whether access or customer trust is involved, and who can safely decide. Use examples at the boundary between levels so a queue does not depend on personal interpretation.

## Role boundary and review

The assistant can collect facts, apply the agreed label, and route the item. The accountable owner handles urgent judgment, changes the response level when evidence changes, and records the reason. Never let a low-confidence label delay a safety, access, or irreversible-change concern.
