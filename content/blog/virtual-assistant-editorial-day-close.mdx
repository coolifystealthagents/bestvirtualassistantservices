# Virtual Assistant Editorial Day-Close Routine

Published September 1, 2026.

Day close prevents unfinished editorial work from becoming invisible overnight.
Use the [content calendar workflow](/blog/virtual-assistant-content-calendar-workflow) to place this control in the daily sequence, the [editorial quality checklist](/blog/virtual-assistant-editorial-quality-checklist) for adjacent review steps, and [NIST small business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber) when the routine handles business information.

## Virtual Assistant Editorial Day-Close Routine: operating steps

Compare the morning plan with the verified publication manifest. Record what shipped, what moved, and why. Do not count a draft as released until its route and metadata checks pass.

## Make blockers actionable

For every unfinished item, name the blocking condition, current owner, required next action, and earliest check-back time. “Waiting” is incomplete unless the record says who or what can change the state.

## Prepare tomorrow's first decisions

Place urgent approvals, expiring sources, and capacity conflicts at the top of the next queue. Confirm that briefs and access are ready before reserving a drafting slot.

## Protect continuity

Write a short handoff for backup coverage, including release identifiers and where evidence is stored. Remove temporary access that is no longer needed and secure any exported working files.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should day close happen at a fixed time?

Use a predictable cutoff that leaves enough time for escalation while the accountable owners are still available.

### What should carry into the next day?

Only work with a clear owner, next action, preserved context, and a newly confirmed priority.
