# Virtual Assistant Customer Escalation Notes

A concise format for escalating customer issues with enough context for a fast, accountable decision.

## Virtual Assistant Customer Escalation Notes: State the issue in one sentence

Start with the customer request, current status, and why the issue needs an owner. Add the source record, relevant dates, attempted response, and the decision needed. Avoid adjectives that imply blame or urgency without evidence. A clean summary lets the reviewer act without reconstructing the whole conversation.

## Preserve the customer voice and the boundary

Quote only the necessary words and link to the complete record in the approved system. A virtual assistant may classify, summarize, and draft a reply, but should not make promises about refunds, policy exceptions, or legal outcomes. The customer support queue guide offers a useful companion for queue status design.

## Close the loop

After a decision, record the owner, response sent, and any change needed in the knowledge base. Review escalations for repeat causes such as unclear forms, missing access, or inconsistent instructions. NIST small-business guidance is relevant when escalation notes include customer data: [NIST small-business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber)

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the owner provide first?

Provide the source of truth, approved examples, the expected output, the reviewer, and a clear rule for exceptions.

### What should remain with the owner?

Keep approvals, policy interpretation, sensitive decisions, and irreversible changes with the responsible owner.
