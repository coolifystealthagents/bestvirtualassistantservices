# Virtual Assistant Donor Record Review

Donor record review should make gaps visible while respecting the sensitivity of the information. A virtual assistant can compare fields and source records, while an authorized owner decides how corrections affect the relationship.

## Virtual Assistant Donor Record Review: compare known fields

Check identity fields, communication preferences, gift history references, and source dates against the approved record. Mark a field missing or conflicting rather than filling it from assumption.

## Separate correction from interpretation

A duplicate record is an observation; the decision to merge it is an accountable action. Keep those steps distinct and retain the evidence for the decision.

## Use an exception queue

Group questions by owner and type so they can be answered efficiently. The [donor records controls guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-donor-records-controls) supports boundaries, while the [CRM data entry guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-crm-data-entry-checklist) supports field hygiene.

## Protect preference data

Do not use a review as permission to contact a person. Handle communication preferences and personal details according to the organization’s rules.

## Limit access and retention

Keep review notes in the approved system and delete unnecessary copies. Consult [NIST small business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber) for practical safeguards.
