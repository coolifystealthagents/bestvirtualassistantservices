# Virtual assistant call quality sampling



## How to manage Virtual assistant call quality sampling

Choose one question before selecting calls: note accuracy, escalation judgment, or confirmation capture. Preserve case references and score what can be observed.

## Role boundary for Virtual assistant call quality sampling

The assistant may tag examples, while the service owner decides whether coaching, a script, or a policy needs adjustment.

## Evidence and handoff

Use the [services library](/services) and [provider vetting](/provider-vetting) to define ownership. For a named reference, consult [FTC privacy and security guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/privacy-security).

## FAQ

### What should the owner record?
The source, reviewer, next action, and the point at which the virtual assistant must stop and escalate.
