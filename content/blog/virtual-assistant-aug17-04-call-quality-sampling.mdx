# Virtual assistant call quality sampling

Define observable criteria for context checks, accuracy, notes, escalation, and close. Sample ordinary, complex, and escalated calls and link every score to an observed moment.

## Applying virtual assistant call quality sampling

Sample for risk, not convenience. Include routine calls, unusual requests, and escalations, then tie each score to a timestamp or observable phrase. A scorecard should distinguish an inaccurate answer from a good escalation made before the caller was promised something.

## Role boundary and review

The assistant can assemble recordings or notes and mark evidence against the rubric. The service owner decides whether a pattern requires coaching, a script change, or a scope boundary. Keep disputed scores separate from confirmed findings so a small sample is not presented as a broad performance claim.

### Sampling record

Keep the sample rationale, rubric version, observed evidence, disputed scores, coaching action, and review date.
