# Virtual Assistant Knowledge Base Article Reviews

An article review should answer whether a reader can find, understand, and safely apply the guidance. It should also show who owns the next correction when information is incomplete.

## Virtual Assistant Knowledge Base Article Reviews: check the promise

Compare the title and opening with the actual reader question. Remove scope that the article cannot support and identify any prerequisite the reader needs.

## Verify the change-sensitive parts

Check dates, links, product names, and stated exceptions against their sources. Record the source and review date rather than claiming permanent accuracy.

## Test findability

Use the terms a reader would search and inspect related links. The [knowledge base search guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-knowledge-base-search-quality) supports discovery, while the [maintenance guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-knowledge-base-maintenance) supports ownership.

## Keep approvals visible

Route changes involving policy, safety, or commitments to the responsible owner. A reviewer can identify the issue without approving the correction.

## Protect internal context

Keep unpublished notes out of public articles. [NIST small business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber) is a useful reference for separating access by need.
