# Virtual assistant SOP change logs

Record version, effective date, approver, evidence, affected queues, and prior-version link. Label superseded copies and sample work after the effective date to catch mixed instructions.

## Applying virtual assistant sop change logs

For virtual assistant sop change logs, begin with the authoritative source and write down the known facts, missing inputs, and due point. Test one ordinary case and one exception before calling the process routine. Keep an unresolved item visible instead of turning an assumption into completion.

## Role boundary and review

In virtual assistant sop change logs, the virtual assistant may gather context, normalize records, identify gaps, and prepare a review queue. A usable handoff names the evidence, open question, next owner, and review date.
