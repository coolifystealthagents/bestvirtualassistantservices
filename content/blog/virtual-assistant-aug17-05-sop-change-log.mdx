# Virtual assistant SOP change logs

Record version, effective date, approver, evidence, affected queues, and prior-version link. Label superseded copies and sample work after the effective date to catch mixed instructions.

## Applying virtual assistant sop change logs

A change log is useful only when it connects an instruction to the work it changes. Capture the old rule, new rule, effective moment, approver, affected queue, and a sample completed under the new version. Do not let a renamed file stand in for an acknowledged change.

## Role boundary and review

The assistant can maintain version references and find work completed during the transition. The process owner approves substantive changes and decides how to handle items started under the prior rule. Retain a short rationale when a change affects quality, access, customer communication, or escalation.
