# Vendor Comparison Notes That Keep Decisions Grounded

A vendor comparison note makes alternatives easier to discuss by separating stated capabilities, evidence, limitations, and questions for the buyer.

## Vendor Comparison Notes That Keep Decisions Grounded begins with the right facts

Use the same headings for every option: relevant service, fit with the stated need, evidence date, dependencies, and unanswered question. Cite the source of each material claim. Use the [work intake guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-work-intake-triage) to state the requested outcome, relevant deadline, and decision owner before details are gathered. A focused brief keeps support work useful without asking for information that does not affect the next step.

## Check context before changing a record

Compare the current record with the approved source and note the date checked. Keep original wording when it carries meaning, and distinguish a confirmed fact from a suggestion or interpretation. If two sources disagree, preserve both references and make the conflict visible.

## Keep authority and uncertainty visible

Do not rank a vendor using invented facts or undisclosed preferences. Do not imply endorsement, certification, or availability without a reliable source. A virtual assistant can prepare information, organize a review list, and point out missing evidence; the owner or designated specialist should make decisions that change commitments, public statements, access, or sensitive records.

## Make the handoff easy to review

Close with the buyer’s decision criteria and a short list of facts still requiring confirmation. A comparison should support judgment, not replace it. A [document management guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-document-management-system) can keep the approved source separate from working notes. For general security considerations when handling business records, consult the [NIST small business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber).

## Questions worth answering before the work is closed

### What is the source of truth?

Name the record or approved document that controls the decision, and record when it was last checked.

### What belongs in an exception note?

State the missing or conflicting fact, the evidence reviewed, the risk of guessing, and who should decide.

### When is the handoff ready?

It is ready when completed items, unresolved questions, source links, and the next decision owner are all clear.
