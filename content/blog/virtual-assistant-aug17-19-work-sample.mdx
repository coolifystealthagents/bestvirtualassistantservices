# Virtual assistant work sample evaluation



## How to manage Virtual assistant work sample evaluation

Define input, expected output, allowed tools, time box, review criteria, and what the candidate should flag. Score accuracy, completeness, judgment, and clarity separately.

## Role boundary for Virtual assistant work sample evaluation

Use equivalent material and do not turn a short evaluation into unpaid production work; a sample supports a bounded inference.

## Evidence and handoff

Use the [services library](/services) and [provider vetting](/provider-vetting) to define ownership. For a named reference, consult [U.S. Department of Labor apprenticeship resources](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/apprenticeship).

## FAQ

### What should the owner record?
The source, reviewer, next action, and the point at which the virtual assistant must stop and escalate.
