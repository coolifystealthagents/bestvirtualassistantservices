# Virtual assistant recurring-work backlogs



## How to manage Virtual assistant recurring-work backlogs

Label recurring items by due date, dependency, effort, and consequence. A long undifferentiated list hides what needs a decision.

## Role boundary for Virtual assistant recurring-work backlogs

Set a review interval and decide whether unanswered items are archived, returned, or escalated instead of leaving them falsely active.

## Evidence and handoff

Use the [services library](/services) and [provider vetting](/provider-vetting) to define ownership. For a named reference, consult [U.S. Digital Service playbook](https://playbook.usds.gov/).

## FAQ

### What should the owner record?
The source, reviewer, next action, and the point at which the virtual assistant must stop and escalate.
