# Virtual Assistant Knowledge Base Maintenance

Published August 31, 2026.

Knowledge-base support succeeds when every article has an owner, evidence, and a review date.

## Virtual Assistant Knowledge Base Maintenance: create an article inventory

Track title, audience, owner, last review, source links, traffic, and known issues. The [service role library](/services) helps establish who coordinates and who approves.

## Triage stale or conflicting guidance

Flag broken links, old screenshots, duplicated procedures, and conflicting instructions. Do not quietly choose which policy is correct.

## Control revisions

Draft changes with a visible change note and route them to the subject owner. Apply account and information controls from the [NIST small business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber).

## Publish and monitor

After approval, record the revision date and check search terms or support questions for new gaps. The [provider vetting checklist](/provider-vetting) helps evaluate documentation support.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the assistant document?

Document the source, action, status, owner, due date, and any exception that needs review.

### When should work stop?

Stop when evidence is missing, instructions conflict, access is inappropriate, or an owner-only decision is required.
