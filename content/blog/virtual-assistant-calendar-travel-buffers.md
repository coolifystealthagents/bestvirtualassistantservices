# Virtual Assistant Calendar Travel Buffers

Travel buffers make availability honest. They should reflect the movement, setup, and recovery time around an appointment rather than being treated as invisible empty space.

## Virtual Assistant Calendar Travel Buffers: identify the journey

Record origin, destination, mode, time zone, and any known access constraint. If the travel estimate is uncertain, label the assumption and leave room for review.

## Protect transitions

Include time for arrival, equipment, and notes when those activities affect readiness. A full calendar is not necessarily a usable calendar.

## Present the effect

When a buffer removes an apparent opening, explain which constraint caused it. The [travel itinerary guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-travel-itinerary-review) helps with trip context, and the [calendar capacity guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-calendar-capacity-planning) supports broader planning.

## Escalate conflicts

Show the conflicting commitments and ask the owner which one takes priority. Do not resolve a material conflict by silently deleting a buffer.

## Treat location data carefully

Share travel details only with people who need them. Review [NIST small business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber) for sensible access controls.
