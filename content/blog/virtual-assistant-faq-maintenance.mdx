# Keeping a Customer FAQ Accurate Over Time

An FAQ earns trust when its answers match the current customer experience and make uncertainty visible.

## Keeping a Customer FAQ Accurate Over Time begins with the right facts

Collect repeated questions, compare each answer with the approved source, and note when an answer depends on account type, location, or another condition. Keep the question in the customer’s language where possible. Use the [work intake guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-project-status-reporting) to state the requested outcome, relevant deadline, and decision owner before details are gathered. A focused brief keeps support work useful without asking for information that does not affect the next step.

## Check context before changing a record

Compare the current record with the approved source and note the date checked. Keep original wording when it carries meaning, and distinguish a confirmed fact from a suggestion or interpretation. If two sources disagree, preserve both references and make the conflict visible.

## Keep authority and uncertainty visible

Do not publish an answer based on a guess or an isolated message. Flag anything involving legal, financial, medical, or account-specific judgment for the responsible reviewer. A virtual assistant can prepare information, organize a review list, and point out missing evidence; the owner or designated specialist should make decisions that change commitments, public statements, access, or sensitive records.

## Make the handoff easy to review

For each proposed change, include the old answer, evidence for the update, affected pages, and a reviewer. Retain a short change note so future questions have context. A [document management guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-work-intake-triage) can keep the approved source separate from working notes. For general security considerations when handling business records, consult the [NIST small business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber).

## Questions worth answering before the work is closed

### What is the source of truth?

Name the record or approved document that controls the decision, and record when it was last checked.

### What belongs in an exception note?

State the missing or conflicting fact, the evidence reviewed, the risk of guessing, and who should decide.

### When is the handoff ready?

It is ready when completed items, unresolved questions, source links, and the next decision owner are all clear.
