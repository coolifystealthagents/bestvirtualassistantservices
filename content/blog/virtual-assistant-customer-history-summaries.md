# Virtual Assistant Customer History Summaries

A good customer history summary lets a service person understand what happened, what was promised, and what remains open. It should preserve sequence and evidence without adding a judgment that the record cannot support.

## Virtual Assistant Customer History Summaries: keep the timeline intact

Start with dates, channels, and the customer’s stated need. Distinguish a request from a response and a response from a completed action. Chronology helps a reviewer find the point where attention is needed.

## Use neutral language

Describe the customer’s words accurately and avoid labels such as difficult or careless. Record the observable issue, the response given, and the unresolved question. Neutral wording travels better between teams.

## Show the current state

End with a clear state such as awaiting customer detail, awaiting internal review, or ready for response. Include the source link and the owner for the next step.

## Limit unnecessary exposure

Include only information needed for the service decision. The [customer feedback summary guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-customer-feedback-summary) offers a useful structure, and the [support queue workflow](/blog/virtual-assistant-customer-support-queue-workflow) helps connect summaries to ownership.

## Review for accuracy

Compare the summary with the source before it is used for a consequential response. For handling access and retention thoughtfully, see [NIST small business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber). A shorter accurate summary is safer than a confident but unsupported one.
