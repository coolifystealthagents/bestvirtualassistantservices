# Virtual Assistant Social Comment Routing

Social comments move quickly, but speed does not remove the need for judgment. Clear routing rules help a virtual assistant identify routine questions while sending complaints, safety concerns, and commitments to the right owner.

## Virtual Assistant Social Comment Routing: classify intent

Separate questions, praise, criticism, spam, and requests for private details. Define what counts as a response-ready question and what requires a reviewer.

## Make urgency observable

Use signals such as a safety concern, a public allegation, or a time-sensitive service issue. Avoid treating volume or strong language alone as proof of urgency.

## Keep response authority clear

Prepare context and a draft only where permitted. The [comment escalation guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-social-comment-escalation) helps with exceptions, and the [social moderation guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-social-media-moderation) supports routine sorting.

## Record the route

Capture the original link, classification, owner, and next review point. This lets the team explain what happened without copying private information into multiple tools.

## Protect public and private data

Move sensitive details to an approved private channel. [NIST small business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber) is a useful reference for access and handling decisions.
