# Preparing a Social Media Calendar With Good Guardrails

A social media calendar can make approved communications easier to coordinate while keeping claims, ownership, and final approval visible.

## Preparing a Social Media Calendar With Good Guardrails begins with the right facts

Organize approved themes, audience, channel, target date, asset reference, and required reviewer. Check that each item has a source for factual claims and a clear owner for replies. Use the [work intake guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-client-onboarding-controls) to state the requested outcome, relevant deadline, and decision owner before details are gathered. A focused brief keeps support work useful without asking for information that does not affect the next step.

## Check context before changing a record

Compare the current record with the approved source and note the date checked. Keep original wording when it carries meaning, and distinguish a confirmed fact from a suggestion or interpretation. If two sources disagree, preserve both references and make the conflict visible.

## Keep authority and uncertainty visible

Do not publish, respond to a sensitive comment, or make a claim about a service without the owner’s approval. Keep draft suggestions distinct from approved copy. A virtual assistant can prepare information, organize a review list, and point out missing evidence; the owner or designated specialist should make decisions that change commitments, public statements, access, or sensitive records.

## Make the handoff easy to review

Hand off a calendar with approval state, missing assets, and questions. The final reviewer should see the context behind each proposed item, not just a list of dates. A [document management guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-project-status-reporting) can keep the approved source separate from working notes. For general security considerations when handling business records, consult the [NIST small business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber).

## Questions worth answering before the work is closed

### What is the source of truth?

Name the record or approved document that controls the decision, and record when it was last checked.

### What belongs in an exception note?

State the missing or conflicting fact, the evidence reviewed, the risk of guessing, and who should decide.

### When is the handoff ready?

It is ready when completed items, unresolved questions, source links, and the next decision owner are all clear.
