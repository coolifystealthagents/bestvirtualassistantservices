# Virtual assistant time-zone handoffs

Record both local clocks, the shared window, cutoff, and next response point. Put status, sources, open decisions, and receiving owner in one packet so a handoff survives a missed overlap.

## Applying virtual assistant time-zone handoffs

For virtual assistant time-zone handoffs, begin with the authoritative source and write down the known facts, missing inputs, and due point. Test one ordinary case and one exception before calling the process routine. Keep an unresolved item visible instead of turning an assumption into completion.

## Role boundary and review

In virtual assistant time-zone handoffs, the virtual assistant may gather context, normalize records, identify gaps, and prepare a review queue. The owner retains decisions involving commitments, sensitive information, public statements, legal interpretation, or irreversible changes. A usable handoff names the evidence, open question, next owner, and review date.
