# Virtual assistant time-zone handoffs

Record both local clocks, the shared window, cutoff, and next response point. Put status, sources, open decisions, and receiving owner in one packet so a handoff survives a missed overlap.

## Applying virtual assistant time-zone handoffs

Write the handoff against elapsed time, not a vague label such as “end of day.” Include the sender's time zone, the receiver's local deadline, the overlap window, and the next non-overlap checkpoint. A dated packet prevents a holiday or daylight-saving change from silently moving the promise.

## Role boundary and review

The assistant may prepare the packet and flag work that cannot reach the receiver before cutoff. The owner keeps decisions about customer commitments and priority changes. When an item crosses midnight for either party, name the effective date and receiving owner rather than relying on a chat timestamp.
