# Virtual Assistant Lead Qualification Notes

For lead qualification notes, begin with the record that makes the next decision possible, then keep the owner and stopping point visible.

## Virtual Assistant Lead Qualification Notes: Record the prospect’s stated need

A qualification note should preserve the person’s own goal, timing, current situation, and requested next step. Avoid translating uncertainty into a sales label too early. The note is useful when another colleague can understand the opportunity without making the prospect start over.

## Distinguish fit from enthusiasm

A friendly conversation does not answer whether the service fits the need, authority, timing, or practical constraints. Capture evidence for each relevant dimension and mark unanswered questions plainly. This produces a fairer handoff than a single hot, warm, or cold judgment.

## Use respectful disqualifiers

If the request is outside the service, say what is missing or mismatched without disparaging the prospect. Record the reason in neutral language and preserve any permission about future contact. A clear boundary protects the prospect’s time and the company’s attention.

## Keep sensitive detail out

Do not place credentials, unnecessary personal information, or confidential customer material in a general lead note. Link to an approved secure record when a specialist needs more context. The FTC’s [privacy and security guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/privacy-security) is a useful reminder to reduce data exposure.

## End with a specific handoff

State the next owner, promised response, open question, and date for follow-up. If the prospect has not agreed to a meeting, do not describe one as scheduled. Accurate notes make a warm introduction feel personal without becoming presumptuous.


## Keep the decision visible

For this topic, preserve the source and route uncertainty to the named owner. Reference: [Federal Trade Commission](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/privacy-security).
