# Virtual Assistant KPI Dashboard Preparation

KPI Dashboard Preparation is useful when it makes a practical decision easier without hiding uncertainty. The goal is a dependable record, not a polished status that conceals the work still waiting.

## Virtual Assistant KPI Dashboard Preparation: the practical question

Completion should be observable. Record the source used, the change made or proposed, the item still waiting, and the person who decides next. Avoid a bare done label. Someone opening the record tomorrow should know whether the work is approved, prepared, or paused.

One final review of kpi dashboard preparation should ask whether the record tells the truth about its state. It should distinguish a verified fact from a proposed correction, a completed action from a waiting action, and a normal case from an exception. Those distinctions are especially important when two reports use one label for different populations.
If the work is handed between people, preserve the source and the question together. A later reviewer should not have to reconstruct why a field was accepted, why a correction was declined, or why an item remained open. This simple record of reasoning protects continuity and makes the service easier to improve.
