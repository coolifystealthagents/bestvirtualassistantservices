# Virtual Assistant Meeting Notes Workflow

Published August 31, 2026.

Meeting notes become useful when they distinguish decisions from discussion and give every action one owner.

## Virtual Assistant Meeting Notes Workflow: prepare the meeting notes structure

Set up headings for decisions, actions, deadlines, open questions, and parked topics before the call. Compare role boundaries in the [service role library](/services) before assigning sensitive follow-up.

## Record facts without inventing certainty

Capture what was said, mark unclear ownership, and flag conflicts for the meeting lead. The assistant should not reinterpret a tentative comment as an approved decision.

## Publish a controlled recap

Send the draft through the named reviewer and retain it in the approved workspace. The [NIST small business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber) is a useful baseline when notes include business information.

## Track actions to closure

Review due items on a fixed cadence, record revised dates, and escalate blocked actions. The [provider vetting checklist](/provider-vetting) can help assess supervision and quality controls.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the assistant document?

Document the source, action, status, owner, due date, and any exception that needs review.

### When should work stop?

Stop when evidence is missing, instructions conflict, access is inappropriate, or an owner-only decision is required.
