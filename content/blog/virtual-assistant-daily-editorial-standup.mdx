# Virtual Assistant Daily Editorial Standup

Published September 1, 2026.

A short editorial standup helps a virtual assistant surface decisions without turning the meeting into another production queue.
Use the [content calendar workflow](/blog/virtual-assistant-content-calendar-workflow) to place this control in the daily sequence, the [editorial quality checklist](/blog/virtual-assistant-editorial-quality-checklist) for adjacent review steps, and [NIST small business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber) when the routine handles business information.

## Virtual Assistant Daily Editorial Standup: operating steps

Before the call, record each article's current stage, owner, promised publish date, and next required action. Link the working brief instead of repeating its contents. This makes the standup a control point rather than a reporting performance.

## Discuss exceptions before routine work

Start with missing sources, overdue reviews, conflicting edits, and access problems. Green items need only a quick confirmation. The sequence protects scarce reviewer time and prevents a blocked draft from hiding behind a long list of normal progress.

## Assign a named next move

Every discussed exception should leave with one owner, one action, and one check-back time. The assistant may coordinate the record, but the editor retains decisions about claims, positioning, and publication readiness.

## Close the loop after the call

Update the editorial board while the decisions are fresh, notify absent owners, and flag any change to the daily publishing plan. Keep meeting notes with the article record so later reviewers can understand why the queue changed.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long should the standup take?

Ten to fifteen minutes is usually enough when status is prepared in advance and discussion stays focused on exceptions.

### What belongs outside the standup?

Detailed editing, keyword debate, and source evaluation should move to a separate working session with the appropriate owner.
