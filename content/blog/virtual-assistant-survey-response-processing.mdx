# Virtual Assistant Survey Response Processing

Published August 31, 2026.

Survey support is reliable when raw responses stay intact and summaries remain traceable to evidence.

## Virtual Assistant Survey Response Processing: preserve the raw survey export

Save an unedited source copy, record the export time, and define the approved analysis question. The [service role library](/services) can help separate research support from decision ownership.

## Apply a written tagging guide

Use defined categories, log ambiguous responses, and test a sample with the reviewer. Do not infer identity, intent, or sentiment beyond the written method.

## Protect respondent information

Minimize copied personal data and restrict access to approved systems. Use the [NIST small business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber) as a baseline for protecting business information.

## Deliver evidence with limits

Report counts, examples, exclusions, and unresolved coding questions. The [provider vetting checklist](/provider-vetting) helps buyers ask about privacy and quality review.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the assistant document?

Document the source, action, status, owner, due date, and any exception that needs review.

### When should work stop?

Stop when evidence is missing, instructions conflict, access is inappropriate, or an owner-only decision is required.
