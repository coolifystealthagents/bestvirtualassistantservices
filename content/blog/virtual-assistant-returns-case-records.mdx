# Organizing Returns Case Records for Faster Review

A returns record gives a support team the facts needed to review a request without losing the customer’s original explanation.

## Organizing Returns Case Records for Faster Review begins with the right facts

Capture order reference, item identity, request date, reason as stated, evidence received, and the current decision owner. Keep status labels tied to a real next step. Use the [work intake guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-project-status-reporting) to state the requested outcome, relevant deadline, and decision owner before details are gathered. A focused brief keeps support work useful without asking for information that does not affect the next step.

## Check context before changing a record

Compare the current record with the approved source and note the date checked. Keep original wording when it carries meaning, and distinguish a confirmed fact from a suggestion or interpretation. If two sources disagree, preserve both references and make the conflict visible.

## Keep authority and uncertainty visible

Do not approve a refund, waive a condition, or state that a carrier is responsible without the authorized policy owner’s decision. Escalate allegations and personal data. A virtual assistant can prepare information, organize a review list, and point out missing evidence; the owner or designated specialist should make decisions that change commitments, public statements, access, or sensitive records.

## Make the handoff easy to review

Present unresolved cases by missing fact and decision needed. Include the customer’s requested outcome and the evidence checked so the reviewer can act without starting over. A [document management guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-work-intake-triage) can keep the approved source separate from working notes. For general security considerations when handling business records, consult the [NIST small business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber).

## Questions worth answering before the work is closed

### What is the source of truth?

Name the record or approved document that controls the decision, and record when it was last checked.

### What belongs in an exception note?

State the missing or conflicting fact, the evidence reviewed, the risk of guessing, and who should decide.

### When is the handoff ready?

It is ready when completed items, unresolved questions, source links, and the next decision owner are all clear.
