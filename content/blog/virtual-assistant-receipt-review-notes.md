# Virtual Assistant Receipt Review Notes

Receipt review is most useful when the note shows what was observed and what still requires an accountable person’s decision. A virtual assistant can organize evidence without approving an unsupported expense.

## Virtual Assistant Receipt Review Notes: capture the essentials

Record the date, merchant, amount as shown, business purpose if supplied, and the source file. Note missing fields instead of guessing them from a familiar merchant name.

## Separate matching from approval

A receipt may match a transaction and still need owner approval. Use distinct states for matched, question open, and ready for approval. This prevents administrative checking from being mistaken for authorization.

## Make questions specific

Ask one question per gap and include the source reference. The [receipt reconciliation guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-receipt-record-reconciliation) supports matching, and the [expense category guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-expense-category-review) helps when classification is the issue.

## Preserve an audit trail

Keep the original document, the review note, and the final decision connected. Do not overwrite a prior explanation when a later correction is made.

## Limit financial exposure

Use appropriate access controls for receipts and transaction records. [NIST small business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber) offers a practical starting point for protecting business data.
