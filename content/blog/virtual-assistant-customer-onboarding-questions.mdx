# Virtual Assistant Customer Onboarding Questions

For customer onboarding questions, begin with the record that makes the next decision possible, then keep the owner and stopping point visible.

## Virtual Assistant Customer Onboarding Questions: Ask only what the next step needs

Onboarding questions work best when each answer has a visible use. Group them by setup, preferences, access, success measure, and support route. Remove curiosity questions that do not change the service. A shorter form is easier to answer accurately and easier for a team to maintain.

## Make the first questions easy

Begin with the customer’s goal and the result they expect to see. Then ask for terminology, important dates, and the people who should be included. Starting with familiar questions builds momentum before the form reaches sensitive or technical details.

## Clarify ownership and approvals

Ask who supplies source materials, who can approve a change, and who should receive status updates. Do not assume the person completing onboarding can make every decision. This distinction prevents a helpful contact from being placed in an authority role they never accepted.

## Handle sensitive information carefully

Explain why a sensitive field is needed, offer a secure channel, and avoid requesting credentials in a general form. Mark fields as optional when they truly are. The [NIST Privacy Framework](https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework) provides a useful lens for connecting data collection with privacy risk management.

## Turn answers into a usable brief

After onboarding, summarize the goal, constraints, open questions, and agreed first milestone. Send the summary for confirmation and record corrections. The final brief should make the customer recognizable to the person helping them without reproducing every answer they gave.


## Keep the decision visible

For this topic, preserve the source and route uncertainty to the named owner. Reference: [National Institute of Standards and Technology](https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework).
