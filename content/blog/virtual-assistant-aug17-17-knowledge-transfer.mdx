# Virtual assistant role knowledge transfer



## How to manage Virtual assistant role knowledge transfer

A role handoff should explain the queue, sources, normal output, review points, exceptions, and escalation contacts. A small supervised sample exposes missing instructions.

## Role boundary for Virtual assistant role knowledge transfer

For each exception, record trigger, safe action, prohibited action, and owner decision; keep preference separate from policy.

## Evidence and handoff

Use the [services library](/services) and [provider vetting](/provider-vetting) to define ownership. For a named reference, consult [NIST small-business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber).

## FAQ

### What should the owner record?
The source, reviewer, next action, and the point at which the virtual assistant must stop and escalate.
