# Virtual Assistant Webinar Coordination Checklist

Published August 31, 2026.

A webinar assistant reduces coordination gaps by keeping one timeline, one owner map, and visible stop conditions.

## Virtual Assistant Webinar Coordination Checklist: create the webinar control sheet

Record platform, audience, speakers, assets, deadlines, rehearsal, moderator, and approval owner. Use the [service role library](/services) to define the coordinator lane.

## Test the attendee journey

Verify approved registration copy, confirmation messages, calendar details, and access links. Escalate claims, pricing, or policy language to the responsible owner.

## Secure the event workspace

Limit host privileges and protect attendee data. The [NIST small business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber) provides a useful account-security baseline.

## Document the final handoff

After the event, record attendance exports, approved recordings, follow-up status, and unresolved issues. The [provider vetting checklist](/provider-vetting) helps evaluate backup and supervision.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the assistant document?

Document the source, action, status, owner, due date, and any exception that needs review.

### When should work stop?

Stop when evidence is missing, instructions conflict, access is inappropriate, or an owner-only decision is required.
