# Virtual assistant capacity planning

Separate calendar blocks, inbox decisions, research packets, and drafted articles because review time differs. Compare accepted completions with arrivals and rework during a busy week before promising a load.

## Applying virtual assistant capacity planning

For capacity planning, measure arrivals and rework separately. A calendar block may be easy to count, while a research packet or article draft consumes review time that is invisible in a task total. Set a provisional ceiling, observe one busy cycle, and lower the promise when the review queue grows.

## Role boundary and review

The assistant can classify work, prepare source packets, and report aging items. The owner decides whether to defer a request, add coverage, or change a deadline. Record accepted output, returned work, and the reason for each exception so the next capacity decision is based on observed load.
