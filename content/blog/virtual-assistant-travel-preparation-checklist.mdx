# Virtual Assistant Travel Preparation Checklist

A practical checklist for organizing itineraries, documents, timing, and contingencies without making unapproved travel decisions.

## Virtual Assistant Travel Preparation Checklist: Confirm the traveler’s brief

Record destination, dates, purpose, preferences, required documents, budget authority, and approval owner. Separate firm constraints from preferences and identify time zones. Do not assume a missing preference or book an option merely because it is available.

## Build an itinerary that can be checked

Show each segment, local time, confirmation reference, transfer buffer, and contact point. A virtual assistant can compare options and prepare a draft; the traveler or owner approves bookings, changes, and unusual risks. The calendar capacity review helps identify conflicts before an itinerary is finalized.

## Prepare a contingency note

List what to do if a connection changes, a document is missing, or a meeting moves. Keep copies in the approved location and avoid putting sensitive passport or payment details in broad notes. NIST small-business guidance can inform protection of travel and account information: [NIST small-business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber)

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the owner provide first?

Provide the source of truth, approved examples, the expected output, the reviewer, and a clear rule for exceptions.

### What should remain with the owner?

Keep approvals, policy interpretation, sensitive decisions, and irreversible changes with the responsible owner.
