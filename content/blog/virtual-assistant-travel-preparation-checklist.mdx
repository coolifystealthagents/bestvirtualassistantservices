# Virtual Assistant Travel Preparation Checklists

For travel preparation checklists, begin with the record that makes the next decision possible, then keep the owner and stopping point visible.

## Virtual Assistant Travel Preparation Checklists: Build around the traveler’s actual trip

Start with destination, dates, purpose, traveler preferences, mobility needs, companions, and the decisions the traveler wants to keep. A generic packing list cannot replace those facts. Confirm the itinerary source before copying details into a checklist.

## Separate fixed facts from choices

Transport, lodging, passport validity, entry rules, and appointment times are facts to verify. Seat preference, meal choice, and optional activities are decisions to collect. Label both types clearly so a preference is not mistaken for a confirmed reservation.

## Use local time deliberately

Write each departure, arrival, transfer, and meeting with city and time zone. Include a buffer for border, airport, or venue constraints where relevant. When daylight-saving changes or overnight travel could confuse the sequence, show the calendar date beside the time.

## Prepare a disruption plan

List cancellation terms, alternative contacts, essential documents, medication needs, and the owner who can approve a change. Do not promise a rebooking or refund without checking the actual provider rule. Preparation is about options, not pretending travel is fully controllable.

## Verify close to departure

Recheck transport, lodging, entry information, weather-sensitive plans, and contact routes before the traveler leaves. For international travel, consult the [U.S. Department of State travel information](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/international-travel.html) rather than relying on an old summary.


## Keep the decision visible

For this topic, preserve the source and route uncertainty to the named owner. Reference: [U.S. Department of State](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/international-travel.html).
