# Virtual assistant vendor renewal preparation

Record agreement, scope, term, notice window, owner, obligations, and authoritative source. The assistant gathers dates and issues; an authorized owner handles legal interpretation and notices.

## Applying virtual assistant vendor renewal preparation

For virtual assistant vendor renewal preparation, begin with the authoritative source and write down the known facts, missing inputs, and due point. Test one ordinary case and one exception before calling the process routine. Keep an unresolved item visible instead of turning an assumption into completion.

## Role boundary and review

In virtual assistant vendor renewal preparation, the virtual assistant may gather context, normalize records, identify gaps, and prepare a review queue. The owner retains decisions involving commitments, sensitive information, public statements, legal interpretation, or irreversible changes. A usable handoff names the evidence, open question, next owner, and review date.
