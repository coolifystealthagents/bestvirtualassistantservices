# Virtual assistant vendor renewal preparation



## How to manage Virtual assistant vendor renewal preparation

Gather agreement, notice date, service history, open issues, and source links with verification dates. Keep facts separate from a renewal recommendation.

## Role boundary for Virtual assistant vendor renewal preparation

Route clauses needing legal interpretation to the qualified reviewer instead of paraphrasing them as certainty.

## Evidence and handoff

Use the [services library](/services) and [provider vetting](/provider-vetting) to define ownership. For a named reference, consult [FTC business guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance).

## FAQ

### What should the owner record?
The source, reviewer, next action, and the point at which the virtual assistant must stop and escalate.
