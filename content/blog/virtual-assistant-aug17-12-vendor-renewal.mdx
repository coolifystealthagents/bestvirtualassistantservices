# Virtual assistant vendor renewal preparation

Record agreement, scope, term, notice window, owner, obligations, and authoritative source. The assistant gathers dates and issues; an authorized owner handles legal interpretation and notices.

## Applying virtual assistant vendor renewal preparation

Start renewal preparation with the agreement and its notice dates, then compare the written scope with what the team actually receives. Flag missing deliverables, changed contacts, unused access, and obligations that require specialist interpretation instead of treating a calendar reminder as a renewal recommendation.

## Role boundary and review

The assistant can assemble the contract record, service history, open issues, and questions for the vendor. An authorized owner decides whether to renew, renegotiate, or seek advice. Keep notice-window evidence and the final decision separate so preparation is not mistaken for legal approval.

Use the [services library](/services) to clarify the operational lane, while the [provider vetting guide](/provider-vetting) helps compare continuity questions. Treat [NIST guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber) as a general access reference, not contract advice.

### Renewal packet

Keep agreement terms, notice evidence, service gaps, open questions, owner decision, and any specialist review.
