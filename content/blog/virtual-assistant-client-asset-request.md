# How to Organize Client Asset Requests

A clear asset request gives clients one understandable list of what is needed, why it matters, and where to place the approved file.

## How to Organize Client Asset Requests starts with a reliable record

Group requests by purpose, name accepted formats, provide an example, and record the date requested. Avoid asking for sensitive material through an unapproved channel. Name the source of truth before work begins, record when it was checked, and make the intended outcome explicit. This keeps a small administrative task from becoming a chain of assumptions.

## Separate preparation from approval

When an asset arrives, record its source, version, and review status. The working list should show what is still missing without exposing private contents in a broad team channel. A virtual assistant can gather details, normalize labels, identify missing fields, and prepare a review queue. The owner or designated specialist should retain decisions that affect commitments, sensitive information, public statements, or irreversible changes.

## Make the handoff easy to review

At handoff, include the completed range, unresolved items, source links, and a short explanation of unusual cases. ## Frequently asked questions

### What should be checked first?

Confirm the source of truth, the intended audience, the required output, and the approval boundary.

### What belongs in an exception note?

State what is unusual, what evidence was checked, what remains uncertain, and who must decide the next step.

### When is the task ready to expand?

Expand only after review shows that the instructions, records, and escalation path work together consistently.
