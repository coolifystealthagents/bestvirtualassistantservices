# Event Vendor Coordination Notes That Keep Details Together

Coordination notes keep venue, delivery, contact, access, and contingency details together so an event owner can spot a gap before it becomes urgent.

## Event Vendor Coordination Notes That Keep Details Together begins with the right facts

Record each vendor’s role, confirmed details, arrival window, contact route, document source, and question owner. Distinguish a proposal from a confirmed arrangement. Use the [work intake guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-client-onboarding-controls) to state the requested outcome, relevant deadline, and decision owner before details are gathered. A focused brief keeps support work useful without asking for information that does not affect the next step.

## Check context before changing a record

Compare the current record with the approved source and note the date checked. Keep original wording when it carries meaning, and distinguish a confirmed fact from a suggestion or interpretation. If two sources disagree, preserve both references and make the conflict visible.

## Keep authority and uncertainty visible

Do not commit the event to a new term, change a delivery instruction, or disclose attendee information without authorization. Escalate conflicting instructions immediately. A virtual assistant can prepare information, organize a review list, and point out missing evidence; the owner or designated specialist should make decisions that change commitments, public statements, access, or sensitive records.

## Make the handoff easy to review

Use one summary per vendor and a separate exception list. This makes the final review about decisions and missing facts instead of searching through messages. A [document management guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-project-status-reporting) can keep the approved source separate from working notes. For general security considerations when handling business records, consult the [NIST small business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber).

## Questions worth answering before the work is closed

### What is the source of truth?

Name the record or approved document that controls the decision, and record when it was last checked.

### What belongs in an exception note?

State the missing or conflicting fact, the evidence reviewed, the risk of guessing, and who should decide.

### When is the handoff ready?

It is ready when completed items, unresolved questions, source links, and the next decision owner are all clear.
