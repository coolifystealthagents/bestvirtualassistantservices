# Virtual Assistant Appointment Reminder Quality Checks

A focused guide to preparing appointment reminders that are timely, accurate, and respectful of customer communication boundaries.

## Virtual Assistant Appointment Reminder Quality Checks: Confirm the reminder source

Use the approved appointment record as the source of truth and verify the customer, date, time zone, location or link, and response method. Define the reminder window and the words that must remain unchanged. If two systems disagree, pause and escalate rather than choosing the more convenient record.

## Keep messages precise

A reminder should make the next action obvious and avoid exposing unnecessary personal information. A virtual assistant can prepare or send an approved template where permission exists, but should route cancellations, medical details, complaints, and unusual requests to the responsible owner. The calendar capacity review helps prevent reminders from being based on a crowded or outdated schedule.

## Measure misses, not just sends

Review bounced messages, wrong times, duplicate reminders, and unanswered confirmations. Record the cause and correct the source or rule that created it. NIST small-business guidance is relevant when contact records are stored or transmitted: [NIST small-business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber)

## Frequently asked questions

### What should the owner provide first?

Provide the source of truth, approved examples, the expected output, the reviewer, and a clear rule for exceptions.

### What should remain with the owner?

Keep approvals, policy interpretation, sensitive decisions, and irreversible changes with the responsible owner.
