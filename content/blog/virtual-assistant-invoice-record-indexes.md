# Virtual Assistant Invoice Record Indexes

An invoice index is a map to evidence, not a substitute for reviewing that evidence. It should show where the invoice came from, what it relates to, and which step remains open.

## Virtual Assistant Invoice Record Indexes: choose traceable fields

Capture vendor, invoice identifier, received date, related project or service, source location, and review state. Keep the original identifier even when a local naming convention is added.

## Connect supporting records

Link purchase requests, delivery evidence, and correspondence where they are needed for review. Do not attach unrelated files simply because they share a vendor.

## Distinguish status from decision

Received, matched, question open, and ready for approval are useful states. Approval belongs to the designated owner and should be recorded separately.

## Review exceptions

Use a specific question for missing details or conflicting records. The [supporting records guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-invoice-supporting-records) explains evidence, and the [document control guide](/blog/virtual-assistant-document-control) helps preserve revisions.

## Limit record access

Use the narrowest workspace that supports the task. [NIST small business cybersecurity guidance](https://www.nist.gov/itl/smallbusinesscyber) provides practical principles for protecting financial records.
