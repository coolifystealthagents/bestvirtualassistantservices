# Operations Reporting by Virtual Assistants: A Research Brief

A weekly report should help an owner decide what to continue, change, or escalate. It should not hide missing data behind polished charts.

The [analytics reporting brief](/research/virtual-assistant-analytics-reporting) adds a focused check for metric definitions and anomalies.

## Define the metric before collecting it

Write the name, formula, population, period, source, owner, and known exclusions. Link reporting work to the [services overview](/services) and the actual workflow rather than a generic dashboard.

## Build three report layers

Layer one is the source register. Layer two is the checked table. Layer three is the short decision summary. OECD and NIST standards resources support clear definitions and reproducible evidence [1][9].

## Reporting table

| Layer | Assistant prepares | Owner reviews | Stop condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Source | Links and extraction date | Authority | Source changed |
| Measure | Formula and values | Definition | Denominator missing |
| Summary | Trend and exceptions | Decision | Causal claim unsupported |

## Make the report accessible

Use headings, labeled tables, text alternatives, and plain language. WCAG provides a baseline for accessible presentation [2].

## Keep privacy and security in the workflow

Aggregate where possible, restrict raw exports, and keep credentials out of the report. Privacy, security, and records guidance support this separation [3][4][5][7].

## Methodology and limitations

This guide synthesizes official standards and guidance reviewed on 2026-08-07. It is a reporting workflow, not accounting, legal, employment, or statistical advice. Trends do not establish causation without a suitable method.

## Key takeaways

- Definitions and exclusions matter as much as the final number.
- Every headline should point to a source and review date.
- An explicit “unknown” is better than an invented trend.

## FAQs

### Can the assistant interpret the trend?

They can describe the observed change and list plausible questions. The owner should decide what action or causal interpretation is warranted.

### What should a weekly handoff contain?

Include checked values, source changes, exceptions, decisions needed, and the first next action for each issue.
