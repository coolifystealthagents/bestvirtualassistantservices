# Customer Inbox Triage by Virtual Assistants: A Research Brief

Inbox triage creates leverage when it makes the next action obvious without giving an assistant authority to promise refunds, interpret policy, or expose private records.

Use the [social moderation brief](/research/virtual-assistant-social-media-moderation) when the queue crosses from customer service into public response handling.

## Define the queue and the handoff

Name labels, response targets, approved templates, required fields, and the owner for each exception. Use the [services overview](/services) to place inbox work beside the business workflow it supports.

## Use a safe triage sequence

Authenticate the source, identify intent, check for sensitive data, apply the documented label, prepare a response, and route anything outside the brief. CISA and NIST guidance favor recognizing suspicious requests and limiting access [3][5].

## Triage table

| Label | Prepare | Owner decides | Stop condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Routine | Approved answer | Sample quality | Missing account context |
| Billing | Gather identifiers | Remedy or credit | Payment data appears |
| Complaint | Summarize facts | Response and remedy | Legal threat |
| Safety | Preserve message | Incident response | Immediate harm |

## Keep personal data to a minimum

Use the smallest record needed for the task, avoid exporting unnecessary messages, and follow the retention and deletion rules set by the client. Privacy guidance should shape the actual workflow, not sit only in a policy folder [1][2].

## Measure accuracy, not just speed

Track correct labels, first-pass acceptance, escalations, reopened tickets, and owner review time. A fast wrong answer is a quality defect.

## Methodology and limitations

This brief uses official privacy, security, accessibility, incident, records, copyright, remote-work, and digital-economy guidance reviewed on 2026-08-07. It is not customer-service, legal, or privacy advice for a specific company.

## Key takeaways

- Write the authority boundary beside every response template.
- Escalate sensitive or irreversible requests before drafting a promise.
- Review a sample of routine items and every exception.

## FAQs

### Should the assistant send every response?

Only if the owner has approved the template, scope, and escalation rule. Otherwise the assistant prepares a draft for review.

### What belongs in a handoff?

Include message identifier, label, facts, customer need, action already taken, decision needed, owner, and due time.
