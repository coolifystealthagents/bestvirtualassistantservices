# Virtual Assistant Project Status Reporting: A Research Brief

Good status reporting makes the current state, evidence, risk, and next decision easy to inspect.

## Define the status packet

Use a reporting period, completed work, remaining work, blockers, risks, decisions needed, and next owner. The [operations reporting brief](/research/virtual-assistant-operations-reporting) provides a recurring format.

## Separate fact from forecast

The [project coordination checklist](/research/virtual-assistant-project-coordination-checklist) can hold task detail. Status should distinguish completed evidence from estimates and unconfirmed assumptions.

| Field | Include | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Progress | Verified output | Vague activity |
| Risk | Trigger and impact | Unsupported certainty |
| Blocker | Owner and unblock action | Hidden dependency |
| Decision | Choice and due date | Implied approval |

## Methodology and limitations

Ten official sources were reviewed on 2026-08-10 across records, accessibility, privacy, security, and remote work. A status packet is a management aid, not a substitute for project governance.

## Key takeaways

- Every status claim needs a verifiable basis.
- Risks need triggers, impact, and an owner.
- Decisions should be explicit and dated.

## FAQs

### How often should status be reported?

Use the cadence agreed by the project owner and increase it when risk or change makes the normal cadence insufficient.

### Should a blocked item be marked complete?

No. Report the blocker, owner, evidence, and exact next action.
