# Virtual Assistant Service Level Reporting: A Research Brief

Published August 31, 2026.

Service-level reports need stable definitions, traceable data, and separate treatment of exceptions.

## Define each measure

Record the start event, stop event, business hours, exclusions, source, target, and owner. The [research library](/research) includes related reporting briefs.

## Validate the reporting dataset

Check missing periods, duplicates, time zones, status changes, and reopened work. The [weekly operations report](/blog/virtual-assistant-weekly-operations-report) helps connect measures to an actionable handoff.

## Show distributions and exceptions

Pair averages with volumes, ranges, aged backlog, and excluded cases so a headline number does not hide operational risk.

## Keep interpretation controlled

The assistant can prepare and check the report, while the service owner explains causes, approves target changes, and chooses corrective action.

## Methodology and limitations

This brief synthesizes official security, privacy, accessibility, records, digital-economy, and remote-work resources reviewed on August 31, 2026. It translates general controls into an operational workflow and does not replace legal, security, financial, or professional advice.

## Key takeaways

- Define the source, method, owner, and stop condition before work begins.
- Verify a risk-based sample and preserve correction evidence.
- Keep consequential decisions with the accountable business owner.
