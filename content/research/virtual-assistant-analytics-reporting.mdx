# Analytics Reporting by Virtual Assistants: A Research Brief

Analytics assistance is strongest when the assistant preserves the path from event to metric to decision. It is weakest when a neat chart conceals a changed definition or incomplete data.

Use the [operations reporting brief](/research/virtual-assistant-operations-reporting) for the weekly handoff and exception layer.

## Start with a measurement brief

Record the question, metric definition, source systems, time zone, filters, refresh schedule, and decision owner. The [research library](/research) shows why methodology belongs in the published artifact.

## Run four quality checks

Check completeness, duplicates, definition changes, and anomalies. Compare a sample against the source system before summarizing. NIST standards and privacy resources support repeatable controls and responsible data handling [1][2].

## Analytics table

| Check | Assistant action | Owner decision | Stop condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Completeness | Flag missing periods | Accept or repair | Source unavailable |
| Definition | Compare documentation | Approve change | Metric changed silently |
| Anomaly | Recheck source and filter | Investigate cause | No evidence for cause |
| Summary | Describe observation | Choose action | Causal claim unsupported |

## Present findings clearly

Pair every chart with a text summary, period, unit, and source. Use accessible headings and tables [3]. Do not publish a public claim that the evidence does not support.

## Access and retention

Keep raw data in approved systems, grant only necessary access, and record the report version and extraction date. Security, privacy, and records sources provide useful control baselines [4][5][6][8].

## Methodology and limitations

This brief uses official standards, privacy, security, accessibility, records, digital-economy, and remote-work resources reviewed on 2026-08-07. It does not provide statistical, financial, or business advice for a particular dataset.

## Key takeaways

- A metric is not stable until its definition and source are stable.
- Investigate anomalies before narrating them.
- Keep data preparation separate from owner decisions about action.

## FAQs

### Can an assistant explain why a metric changed?

They can list verified changes in inputs, filters, or timing. A causal explanation needs evidence and owner review.

### How should a dashboard be handed off?

Provide the source, refresh time, definitions, exceptions, version, and decision questions in the same package.
