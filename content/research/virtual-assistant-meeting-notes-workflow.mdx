# Virtual Assistant Meeting Notes Workflow: A Research Brief

Meeting notes create operational value when participants can distinguish what was said, what was decided, and what still needs confirmation.

## Capture the meeting contract

Record the meeting purpose, attendees, date, permitted recording or transcript source, and distribution list. The [handoff brief](/research/virtual-assistant-knowledge-transfer-handoffs) adds ownership rules.

## Draft in three states

Start with factual notes, convert explicit decisions into a decision log, and list follow-ups with owners and dates. The [project coordination checklist](/research/virtual-assistant-project-coordination-checklist) supports the action register.

| State | Required fields | Release test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Notes | Speaker or source, time, topic | Source is retained |
| Decision | Decision, scope, approver | Approver is explicit |
| Action | Owner, due date, next step | Owner confirms |

## Methodology and limitations

Ten official sources on records, privacy, security, accessibility, and remote work were reviewed on 2026-08-10. Local policy and consent requirements still govern each organization.

## Key takeaways

- Keep factual notes separate from interpretation.
- Every action needs an owner and a next step.
- Confirm sensitive distribution before sending.

## FAQs

### Can an assistant infer decisions?

They can identify proposed decisions, but should label them as pending until an authorized participant confirms them.

### How long should notes be retained?

Follow the organization’s records schedule and document the applicable category.
