# Virtual Assistant Email Queue Prioritization: A Research Brief

Email triage is a classification task, not permission to make unapproved commitments. A clear queue model makes exceptions visible.

## Set priority dimensions

Classify urgency, business impact, sensitivity, and required owner. The [inbox triage workflow](/research/virtual-assistant-inbox-triage-workflow) gives a related operating pattern.

## Route with stop conditions

The [customer inbox brief](/research/virtual-assistant-customer-inbox-triage) separates routine replies from matters needing an owner. Privacy and security sources support least-access handling.

| Queue | Assistant action | Owner handoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Routine | Label and draft | Review if commitment exists |
| Time-sensitive | Flag with deadline | Named owner immediately |
| Sensitive | Restrict and route | Authorized owner only |
| Unclear | Preserve context | Clarify before reply |

## Methodology and limitations

The model synthesizes ten official sources reviewed on 2026-08-10. It does not replace organizational retention, legal, or incident-response procedures.

## Key takeaways

- Priority needs explicit dimensions and examples.
- Sensitive messages should be routed with minimum necessary access.
- Uncertainty is an escalation state, not a reason to guess.

## FAQs

### May an assistant delete low-value email?

Only when an approved retention and deletion rule explicitly permits it.

### What should a triage log contain?

Capture timestamp, category, owner, action, exception, and source context without copying unnecessary sensitive content.
