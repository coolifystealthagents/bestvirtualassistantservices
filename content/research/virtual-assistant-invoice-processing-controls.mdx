# Virtual Assistant Invoice Processing Controls: A Research Brief

Invoice support can improve consistency when assistants capture and check records while authorized owners retain approval authority.

## Separate the workflow stages

Define intake, field extraction, purchase-order or contract match, exception routing, approval, and retention. The [bookkeeping controls brief](/research/virtual-assistant-bookkeeping-support-controls) covers adjacent boundaries.

## Check before routing

Compare supplier, date, amount, currency, terms, and duplicate indicators against approved records. The [data entry controls brief](/research/virtual-assistant-data-entry-quality-controls) provides a sampling model.

| Check | Assistant action | Owner decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Completeness | Flag missing fields | Request correction |
| Match | Compare approved record | Resolve mismatch |
| Duplicate | Search prior entries | Confirm or reject |
| Approval | Route evidence | Approve or decline |

## Methodology and limitations

Ten official records, security, privacy, accessibility, and work-practice sources were reviewed on 2026-08-10. This is not accounting, tax, or legal advice.

## Key takeaways

- Capture and approval should be separate responsibilities.
- Exceptions need evidence and an owner.
- Retain the source and processing trail according to policy.

## FAQs

### Can an assistant approve an invoice?

Only if an organization has explicitly authorized that role and control separation remains adequate.

### What should happen when a duplicate is suspected?

Pause routing, preserve both records, and ask the designated owner to resolve the match.
