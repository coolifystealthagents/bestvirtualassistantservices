# CRM Data Hygiene by Virtual Assistants: A Research Brief

CRM hygiene is not the same as filling every blank. It means making records accurate enough for the approved workflow while preserving provenance and consent.

The [pipeline handoff brief](/research/virtual-assistant-sales-pipeline-handoffs) shows how clean records support the next owner decision.

## Write the field dictionary first

Define required fields, allowed values, source priority, duplicate rules, date format, and the owner of each decision. The [sales pipeline support page](/services/sales-pipeline-support) is a useful place to connect the fields to real handoffs.

## Check four dimensions

Review completeness, validity, consistency, and freshness. Never infer a lead’s budget, identity, consent, or qualification from a vague note. NIST privacy guidance supports documenting purpose and data handling [1].

## Hygiene table

| Check | Assistant action | Owner action | Stop condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Duplicate | Flag likely matches | Merge decision | Identity is uncertain |
| Missing | Request approved field | Decide required data | Sensitive data requested |
| Stale | Add review flag | Confirm status | No authoritative source |
| Conflict | Record both values | Resolve conflict | Material decision depends on it |

## Protect the system and the record

Use named access, least privilege, and an audit trail. Do not export the entire CRM for convenience. Security and records guidance support limiting access and preserving accountable changes [3][5][7].

## Quality review

Sample normal updates weekly and review every merge, deletion, consent change, and ownership change. Measure correction rate, duplicate flags, unresolved conflicts, and time to resolve.

## Methodology and limitations

This brief draws on official privacy, security, accessibility, records, remote-work, and digital-economy resources reviewed on 2026-08-07. It does not determine compliance, consent, or sales qualification for a particular business.

## Key takeaways

- A field dictionary is the foundation of consistent CRM work.
- Preserve the source and reason for every material change.
- Keep merging, deletion, qualification, and consent decisions with the owner.

## FAQs

### Can an assistant merge duplicates?

They can identify likely duplicates and prepare evidence. The owner should approve a merge when identity or history could be affected.

### What is a good first test?

Use dummy records containing a duplicate, conflicting source, missing consent field, and an item that should be escalated.
